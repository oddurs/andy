The tree view, made honest.

Three columns compete for attention on every row of the browser and the loudest
of them is the least trustworthy: the bar is normalised per level, so a 637M
row and a 7.5G row both draw full. The path column repeats what the label
already said. And the safety rating -- the thing you are actually deciding on --
is everywhere except the list you decide from.
