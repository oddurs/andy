Shading.

The information is right and the surface is flat. Five of the eight roles carry
no colour at all, so nearly every pixel is one foreground at three weights; the
bar, the largest graphic element on every row, has neither colour nor a track;
and the one coloured character per row is yellow on 76% of rows, which spends
colour on the norm and leaves the exceptions no louder than anything else.

Terminals offer more than andy is taking: a theme's ANSI 8 is a true middle
tone -- 3.7 to 4.2 against the background in every theme this was measured on,
against 10.7 to 12.4 for the text -- and dim turns any hue into a tint. This
milestone spends both, without adding a single new meaning.
