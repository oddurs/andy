Full cross-platform, not just "it runs".

v1.1 taught andy the Linux filesystem layout and proved the scan works there.
That is not the same as working on Linux. A developer who sets XDG_CACHE_HOME
sees nothing; a minimal container without ncurses cannot run `andy --json` at
all; a C locale crashes the report. v1.2 closes the distance between running
and working.
