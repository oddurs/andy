Correctness, speed and reach.

andy 1.0 maps developer storage on macOS accurately enough to act on, from one
dependency-free file. v1.1 does not change what it is for. It makes the numbers
provably right, makes the measurement fast enough that the interactive view is
useful from the first second, widens what it recognises, and lets it run on
Linux — where the same caches, toolchains and build trees are just as fat.
