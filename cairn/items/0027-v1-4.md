From inventory to decision.

andy answers "where did my disk go" well and "what should I do about it"
badly. It knows a safety rating for every location and never shows one in the
summary; it prints reclaim commands with `<project>` left for you to fill in
when it knows the path; it elides paths in the middle, where the informative
part is. This milestone is about the distance between a number and an action.
